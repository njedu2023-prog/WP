# WP V20 Hierarchical Opportunity Protocol

## Status and objective

This protocol was frozen before the V20 result was produced.

V20 has one objective: maximize the probability that an executable stock first
published from 14:20 through 14:50 earns a positive net return when sold under
the fixed T+1 close-auction contract after all established costs, slippage and
non-fill penalties. Multiple stocks may qualify and the human user chooses
which one to buy. `NO_SIGNAL` is valid and the system must never weaken a rule
to force a list.

V20 is research-only. The historical interval has already been examined by
earlier versions, so a passing result can freeze a future shadow candidate but
cannot authorize production.

## Independent hypothesis

V10 through V19 tried to solve opportunity selection and stock selection with
one stock-level selector. V19 showed that simply widening the stock pool is not
the missing mechanism. V20 separates the decision:

1. The immutable V9 OOS model ranks the executable stock universe at each tail
   slot.
2. The three highest-ranked stocks per slot form a causal leader stream.
3. A second-stage gate estimates whether each current leader belongs to a
   favorable overnight opportunity regime.
4. A fixed frequency policy controls when that gate may publish candidates.

The second stage is trained only from V9 predictions that were already OOS and
strictly earlier than its current outer test fold. It does not refit V9 or use
future returns to construct the leader stream.

## Inputs and causal features

The immutable source is the complete V9 full-universe walk-forward prediction
set for folds 1 through 22. V20 uses pre-evaluation OOS folds as causal
second-stage training history, while all reported metrics remain restricted to
`20230727` through `20260724`.

At every date and slot, the leader identities are determined only by the V9
`selection_score`, with `ts_code` as a deterministic tie-breaker. Changing T+1
labels or net returns must not change those identities.

The opportunity gate may use only information available by the current slot:

- frozen V9 probability, utility, downside, fill and uncertainty estimates;
- full-universe same-slot market context computed before retrieval;
- label-independent retrieval-source indicators;
- current leader rank, score gap and same-slot leader distribution;
- the same stock's leader appearances and scores from the current or earlier
  tail slots.

Changing a 14:50 input must not change any 14:20 through 14:45 feature.
T+1 truth, later slots and future daily aggregates are forbidden features.

## Fixed model and policy

There is exactly one policy specification:

- top three V9 leaders per slot;
- 126 prior trading days for gate training;
- two-day purge;
- 42 later prior trading days for probability and return calibration;
- final two-day purge before the outer test fold;
- fixed target candidate-day rate: 18%;
- fixed maximum candidates per day: two.

The frequency threshold is the score of the target-ranked daily maximum in the
42-day prior calibration window. Outcomes do not choose this threshold.
Neither the 18% rate nor daily capacity may be changed after viewing V20.

Every candidate must additionally satisfy:

- V9 severe-loss probability no greater than 40%;
- lower round-trip fill probability at least 95%;
- opportunity-model probability disagreement no greater than 30%;
- market-data age no greater than seven minutes when present.

Signals are processed in actual order from 14:20 through 14:50. A stock is
recorded at its first qualifying slot. A later score cannot replace its signal
time or price. Earlier valid candidates consume daily capacity before later
candidates.

## Nested evaluation

Every reported candidate is generated by a gate fitted only on earlier OOS
leader rows. No selected test return enters model fitting, calibration,
threshold calculation or feature construction.

The denominator for candidate-day frequency is every A-share source trading
day in the evaluation window, including days on which the policy emits
`NO_SIGNAL`.

The fixed economic contract is:

- entry: next five-minute close plus established slippage;
- exit: fixed T+1 close-auction order contract;
- baseline all-in costs: the resolved V9 execution configuration;
- stress: an additional 50 basis points per executed candidate;
- failed exit: the established non-fill penalty.

## Historical acceptance gates

The combined nested OOS stream must pass every gate:

- at least 50 candidates and 30 candidate days;
- candidate-day rate from 10% through 25%;
- win rate at least 55%;
- Wilson and day-clustered win-rate lower bounds at least 48%;
- mean net return at least 0.20%;
- positive day-clustered mean-return lower bound;
- profit factor at least 1.20;
- nonnegative mean after an additional 50 basis points;
- 10th-percentile net return at least -3.00%;
- at least two profitable active calendar years;
- worst active calendar-year mean no worse than -0.20%;
- complete source and temporal integrity.

A failed gate rejects V20. No threshold repair or second-best rule is permitted
after the result.

## Future shadow requirement

Even if every historical gate passes, the exact bundle and fixed policy remain
research-only until they complete at least:

- 150 future A-share trading days;
- 60 immutable verified candidates;
- 30 candidate days;
- the same profitability, cost, tail-risk and integrity gates.

Historical replay, reconstruction and backfill never count as future shadow
evidence. Production promotion is always a separate explicit decision.
